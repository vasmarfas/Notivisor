## 1. What the App Does

Notivisor mirrors notifications from an Android phone to a paired VR headset over a direct Bluetooth
or Wi-Fi connection between the two devices. It has no account system, no backend service, and no
cloud component of ours — one app installs on both devices and forwards notifications directly
between the pair you configure.
